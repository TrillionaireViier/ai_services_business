PILLAR 01
How LLMs "See" Companies
  • Online Mentions & Breadth: LLM systems and search indexes see companies better when there are frequent online mentions of the name, niche, geography, and products (including in comments and forum discussions).
  • Consistent Descriptions: Clear, unified messages across social media accounts specifying "who we are / what we do / who it’s for".
  • Supporting Proofs: Case studies, reviews, interviews, and citable materials (user stories aggregated into authoritative case studies).
PILLAR 02
AI Answer Sources & Indexing
  • Structured Publishing: Pages with clear structure, authorship, dates, and comprehensive FAQs get picked up more often in AI answer sources.
  • Citable Materials: Comparative roundups, expert reviews, and comprehensive guides used as "evidence-based sources".
  • Reddit as a Content Bridge: Reddit is exceptionally well-indexed by LLMs and acts as a bridge for your content to be picked up by articles, directories, and reviews.
PILLAR 03
Reputation Signals & Citation Boosting
  • Reliable AI Proof: Trustpilot/review sites, media mentions, and forum discussions give LLMs far more reliable support than your own Instagram post.
  • Multi-Channel Interaction: It’s critical to work not only with your own social accounts, but also with how third-party users discuss your brand online.
