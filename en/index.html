Yes. Colors, fonts, and logo are included in every package. Fully custom design is ordered separately.

You do. The domain is registered to you, and the site code can be taken at any time.

50% at the start, 50% after approval of the finished site. We accept cards and bank transfers.

Minor edits are done within support. For major improvements, we agree on a separate cost in advance.
